Endlessly curious, I always have to try everything. When I found this on the secondary market for next to nothing, I had to give it a try.

**Challenge: 2/5**

The box says two stars, and two stars is accurate. At no point did I feel challenged. It was more like building with LEGO, just assemble the pieces.

**Enjoyment: 3/5**

Cute and short. I colored the edges of the rose but left the greens and browns alone.

**Engineering and Fit: 3/5**

Everything fit well, but let's face it, there was not that much to it. The flexible pieces did not require any extreme bends and went into place easily. I did not need a single drop of glue.

**Materials and Finish: 4/5**

The wood was good. I am not a fan of using this much cardboard, but it works here, and the colors looked good. There were a surprising number of extra parts. I suspect they simply had plenty of room left on the boards.

**Instructions: 3/5**

One large sheet with no preview of the parts needed for each step.

**Display Appeal: 3/5**

Just okay.

**Value: 5/5**

I found this on the secondary market and got a good price. It is not an expensive model to begin with.

**Overall Rating: 3/5**

It is not my cup of tea, but it was a quick and easy build. I think I could have built it while standing in line at the DMV. It could have been more intricate, but it would be a good model for introducing someone to the hobby.

I presented it to my wife when I finished, and she did not swoon. The box promised "a chance encounter between flowers and love." That was not my experience.
