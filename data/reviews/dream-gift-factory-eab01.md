Well, this one made me work for my Christmas magic!

The Dream Gift Factory has gears, tiny pieces, moving parts, lights, a spinning Christmas tree, and enough mechanical mischief to keep me thoroughly occupied. There were moments when the challenge wandered dangerously close to frustration, but the finished factory is absolutely worth it.

**Challenge: 5/5**

Lots of gears, lots of little pieces, and plenty of places where concentration was required. This is not a quick afternoon build, but the challenge makes the finished result much more satisfying.

**Enjoyment: 4/5**

I enjoyed the build, although a few difficult fits and fragile pieces interrupted my happy building trance. There may have been some muttering at the workbench, but Christmas spirit eventually prevailed.

**Engineering and Fit: 4/5**

The gear system was spot-on, and everything works beautifully. As always, if two pieces of wood move against each other, I wax them. Those gears are going to have a long and well-lubricated life.

Some of the assembly fits were challenging. I also spent far too much time trying to eliminate a wobble in the Christmas tree, only to discover that the topper above the star helped stabilize it. Once everything was assembled, the factory came alive exactly as intended.

**Materials and Finish: 3/5**

A couple of the boards were extremely thin, and I had more broken pieces than usual, including two that broke while I was sanding them. It is possible that my sanding technique was a little too enthusiastic for such delicate wood.

Fortunately, the kit included a wonderful supply of spare pieces. Every time something broke, a replacement was waiting. No emergency reconstruction was required!

There was also some splintering that added to my painting duties. A few pieces were colored on only one side even though both sides remained visible, while other beautifully colored surfaces disappeared inside the model forever. Curious choices, but nothing a little paint could not correct.

**Instructions: 5/5**

Detailed, clear, and easy to follow, especially for a model with this many moving parts. No complaints here.

**Display Appeal: 6/5**

Yes, the scale only goes to five. The Dream Gift Factory does not care.

It is absolutely gorgeous and belongs in the same special category as the Coffee Factory. The lights, movement, colors, and layers of detail make it something people will stop and examine. I fully expect it to become a favorite with the grandchildren.

**Value: 5/5**

This is not an overly expensive kit considering the build experience and the finished display. It can be packed away after Christmas, brought out every year, and become part of the family's holiday traditions.

I also appreciate the USB power. No annual battery hunt before the factory can reopen for Christmas!

**Customization**

Naturally, I could not leave everything exactly as designed.

I changed several colors, painted visible unfinished surfaces, and added some holiday figures. Many of the plain wooden pieces received stain, which gave them a warmer and more finished appearance.

There is also a teddy bear in the chimney and, of course, a dog. No customized build of mine is complete without one.

**Overall Rating: 5/5**

The Dream Gift Factory tested my patience in a few places, but the finished result erased most of those memories. It is colorful, mechanical, beautifully illuminated, and filled with Christmas personality.

This is more than another model for the shelf. It is something I expect to bring out every Christmas for years to come!
