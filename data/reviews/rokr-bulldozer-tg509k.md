I have seen these construction vehicles around, and this is definitely my kind of build. It is rated two stars, ages 8+, with 211 pieces, so I figured it would be a quickie.

**Challenge: 3/5**

It says two stars, but I would put it closer to three or even three and a half. It has a lot of moving parts and some very thin wood that requires care. I am sure there are eight-year-olds who could put this together, but at that age I would have turned it into sawdust and ruined a very nice little model.

**Enjoyment: 5/5**

This was fun from start to finish. I like the subject, all the movable parts, and the way everything came together.

I planned to color all the yellow edges yellow, but neither my markers nor my paint was doing it for me. After five coats with very little progress, I switched to black and silver. I enjoyed deciding which yellow pieces would get silver edges and which would get black. I like the finished result, so I guess I made the right choices.

**Engineering and Fit: 4/5**

This is a well-thought-out kit. All the tabs fit, and I liked how the moving parts were designed. I do have to say that several areas needed wax, and that should have been accounted for. Maybe it works without wax, but I will never know. If wood moves against wood, I wax it.

**Materials and Finish: 4/5**

The plywood panels were in good shape. I had one break near the end that was my fault, along with some splintering on the thinnest panel, but neither was a deal breaker. The painted panels looked very good, and the yellow, black, and silver color scheme works perfectly for the model.

**Instructions: 3/5**

Newspaper-style sheet, with no parts list before each step. I am already on record about my dislike of this format. Nothing else to see here, move along.

**Display Appeal: 5/5**

It is a sharp-looking model and will find a place with my other vehicles. If I come across another one from this line, it will probably join it.

**Value: 5/5**

I found this on the secondary market and got a good price. I am not sure whether it is still available anywhere else.

**Overall Rating: 4.5/5**

It is not perfect, but it is a very enjoyable build. It was not as quick as I expected because there are a lot of tiny pieces, but it was not an especially long build either. I will certainly keep an eye out for the others in this series.
