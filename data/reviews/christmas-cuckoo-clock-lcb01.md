I finally finished it. I found this model quite fiddly. Getting the cuckoo clock mechanism working and keeping it working was an adventure, but I am now an expert. If you build this one and get stuck, I am there for you.

The model went into time-out a couple of times so I could think about it. The base construction was fine, the fit was generally good, and only a few places needed glue for reinforcement.

Santa did not come out every time. He seemed to have a mind of his own and a tendency to get stuck. After the finished clock had been on the wall for several weeks, the original batteries finally died. Once I replaced them, Santa began rocketing all the way around every hour. My theory is that I ran the first batteries down while fussing with the mechanism, leaving too little power for him to complete the trip in the finished clock.

I colored the edges, added a dog, of course, and replaced the elf in the chimney with a teddy bear as a salute to Happyduke Toys.

This one made me work, but the finished clock was worth the trouble and became a family favorite.
