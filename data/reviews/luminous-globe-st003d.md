I have always remembered the Monocular Telescope fondly, so I was surprised it took me this long to build another model from the Curious Discovery Series.

This was another build for someone else, this time my brother-in-law, who has a globe collection in his office. My daughter works for him and suggested I build this one as a gift. I hoped he would like it.

**Challenge: 4/5**

This four-star kit contains 185 pieces and had a delicious amount of challenge. I spent about seven hours on it and enjoyed what it demanded from me. There were difficult moments, but they were manageable. I would rate it closer to 4.5 stars.

Interestingly, I had little trouble with what is commonly presented as the main difficulty, the plastic globe panels. Other parts challenged my dexterity more.

**Enjoyment: 5/5**

I loved every minute of this build. There was no real frustration, just the right balance of challenge and quality construction. The kit never got in my way. It simply tested my skills.

**Engineering and Fit: 5/5**

Everything was well engineered and fit correctly, with nothing too loose or too tight. I used no glue at all, and I cannot remember the last time I could say that. I am usually paranoid about something coming loose later where I cannot reach it, so I often glue even when the instructions do not call for it.

**Materials and Finish: 5/5**

The plywood panels were thick, strong, and high quality. Nothing should break if reasonable care is used, and I encountered no splintering.

The wood accepted my antique stain beautifully. I originally planned to go back over several parts with brass paint, but the stained grain looked good enough that I left them alone. I wanted an antique appearance, and I think I achieved it.

**Instructions: 3/5**

This was definitely the weak point. There was no parts list before each step, and the giant, unwieldy pages were less convenient than a booklet. An early electronics test briefly improved my opinion, but the instructions quickly went downhill again.

Several diagrams failed to show critical views or were confusing. I resorted to YouTube build videos and was amused to discover that I was not the only person who struggled in the same places.

**Display Appeal: 5/5**

I achieved the antique look I wanted, and this is a great-looking model. I was proud to give it to my brother-in-law.

**Value: 4/5**

At about $60, it is reasonably priced. I am not convinced I would buy the motorized version again because this is ultimately a static display piece, and I am not sure the motor adds much. You can spin the globe yourself.

**Overall Rating: 5/5**

This was a great building experience. I enjoyed the challenge, and it reminded me how much I liked the Curious Discovery Series. I need to see whether more of them are still being produced.

It is an attractive display piece that can fit into many settings. Almost everyone likes a globe, and the small details draw you in.
