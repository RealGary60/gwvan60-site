**Challenge: 2/5**

This was a single-board kit without many pieces.

**Enjoyment: 4/5**

Most of my enjoyment came from painting it.

**Engineering and Fit: 3/5**

The fit was too loose, and the pieces remained wobbly. I was hoping for tiny Japanese precision engineering and did not get it.

**Materials and Finish: 3/5**

Everything came on one board, and the wood was fairly splintery.

**Instructions: 3/5**

The instructions consisted of four exploded diagrams, but the model was not complicated enough to require much more.

**Display Appeal: 1/5**

Despite a nice painting effort, the roof lines would not remain straight. My vision of a tiny pagoda for the shelf did not materialize.

**Value: 4/5**

I bought eight of these kits for under $10 while I was already paying for shipping through ZenMarket. It provided about an hour of amusement for roughly a dollar.

**Overall Rating: 3/5**

I am not ready to pass judgment on the entire eight-kit set until I build a few more of them.
