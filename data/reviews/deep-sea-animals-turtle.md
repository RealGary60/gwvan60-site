**Challenge: 4/5**

This was a very satisfying little build. It had the smallest nuts and bolts I had worked with at that point, but my old sausage fingers got the job done.

**Enjoyment: 4/5**

I enjoyed the challenge of the tiny hardware and the change of pace from wooden kits.

**Engineering and Fit: 5/5**

Everything fit as intended, and I had no mechanical problems.

**Materials and Finish: 4/5**

I always feel as though these kits should include more spare hardware, but I got it done without losing anything important. I did not even have to search the rug under my desk this time.

**Display Appeal: 4/5**

It will look good on my tiny steampunk creature shelf, but it needs to be positioned so the entire model can be seen. I may need to think about mounting it.

**Value: 4/5**

The series was on sale at seven kits for $120, which is comparable to similar metal kits. This particular model had fewer pieces and was a very quick build.

**Overall Rating: 4/5**

I really like these Moyustore kits. I had four more in the series waiting, and after completing those it would be time to consider one of the larger models.
