**Challenge: 1/5**

I invented my review rating system while building this kit. It required almost no concentration.

**Enjoyment: 1/5**

Foam and cardboard are not my favorite materials. I spent most of the build wanting to get it over with.

**Engineering and Fit: 2/5**

The fit was generally good, but I had to glue several pieces that did not hold securely. It was also hardly an engineering challenge. There were no moving parts, lights, or mechanisms.

**Materials and Finish: 3/5**

The materials are what they are, but the bright colors look good.

**Display Appeal: 2/5**

It is huge. I like the colors and the theme, but I understand that this type of material does not lend itself to a small finished model.

**Value: 0/5**

At about $20 from H Mart, I could have bought a much more satisfying wooden or metal model for the same price and been happier to display the finished product.

**Overall Rating: 1/5**

This may be someone else's cup of tea, but it is not for me, and I will not build another kit like it. You have to try everything once, right?
