This is old-school ROKR. I had some difficulties, but I suspect they were caused more by age and storage conditions than by the original design. This was not the first older eBay kit I bought with brittle wood and extremely tight fits. I am reviewing the kit I actually built, but another builder's experience may be different.

**Challenge: 3/5**

When the pieces cooperate, this is not a complex model. There are only a few gears, and the build is not especially large. The box rates it at three stars and ages 8+, which makes sense only if you do not encounter dry, brittle wood.

**Enjoyment: 4/5**

I always enjoy painting, so the build started well, and I had fun choosing colors. The early portion, mostly the cab, was more frustrating. Once I began waxing every tab, everything fit much better, and I enjoyed the rest of the build.

**Engineering and Fit: 3/5**

The mechanism is not especially complicated, and the fit on my kit was awful. Again, I blame improper storage of an older model, which left the wood dry, splintery, and distorted. Once I defaulted to waxing every tab, assembly improved considerably, but the early breaks were not what I normally expect from ROKR.

**Materials and Finish: 2/5**

The dry, brittle wood seemed ready to break if I looked at it wrong. That may not have been the model's fault. I assumed the solar panel worked, but it would not stop raining long enough for me to test it.

I also found it strange that there was no switch. Do you leave it in the sun and walk away? What if it leaves on its own?

**Instructions: 4/5**

These were standard ROKR instructions. A couple of the perspectives were odd, and I had trouble determining how certain pieces aligned, but there was nothing horrible.

**Display Appeal: 4/5**

It is an attractive model. Mine came out rough-looking, which suited the direction I took with the paint. I wanted an old working vehicle rather than a showroom model. It fits comfortably among my steampunk decorations.

**Value: 2/5**

This was my fault. I wanted the kit and paid $35 on eBay, more than I would usually pay, knowing that older kits can be an adventure.

**Overall Rating: 3/5**

It was worth the five or six hours I spent building it, but it is not a model whose construction or finished result I will especially treasure.
