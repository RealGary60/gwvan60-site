My review

Family picked this up for me at Walmart figured at 12.97$ it was worth a try. It was only a 124 pieces, not a complicated build, but how refreshing. Every tab was perfect, not too loose, not to tight, never touched the sandpaper. Glue provided but it was only for a few pieces that required it. Nothing broke, came out of forms clean, quality mdf, a lot of it printed on both sides. Wiring was simple and easy.

I like customizing, I like painting, I like complexity, but this was just fun. I found myself admiring the engineering as everything fit so well. Could have done the edges but that was too much to ask of my partial colorblindness, too many colors.

I'll probably pick up some more of their kits for breaks between challenging builds (or in the middle in this case). I'd certainly be interested if they did bigger kits.
