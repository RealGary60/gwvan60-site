Gary's review

This was the third of four customized book nooks I built as Christmas gifts, this one for my oldest daughter. It is not the Halloween witch theme the designers intended. Instead, I crammed it full of things personal to one of my favorite children.

The build itself was a bit of an adventure. I did not trust a good portion of it to remain together, so the Titebond came out for reinforcement. There were structural modifications, some required by my oversized decorations and some caused by questionable engineering decisions. The gummy bears and snow globe in the tower were nonnegotiable despite the logistical problems they created.

The clock is powered by its own battery, and the roof must be removed to replace it. The clock also has no accessible off switch, so I hoped the battery would hold out until December 25.

I will not talk about the tower other than to say it was an adventure.

I now have a collection of spare magic and witch-themed pieces that I did not use, so perhaps I will add a Halloween theme to something else. I also chose the fourth and final customized Christmas gift, Sunset Alley, which I planned to rearrange around another daughter's interests.
