My review

My first build from Fifijoy, and I was impressed.

The lack of flat printed accessories was refreshing, and the finished model is amazingly detailed. The materials were high quality, and the MDF pieces snapped together with that satisfying crunch. There was a lot of gluing, but it was intentional assembly rather than glue compensating for poor fit or broken pieces.

The electronics were smooth and easy to install. The acrylic details stepped the model up another level, particularly the tiny darts in the dartboard and the front sign. I also love the mirrored back bar filled with bottles and the hanging wine and glass rack.

The front door needs a handle or doorknob. I checked and do not believe I missed a step or part, so I planned to make something myself. I also kept looking at the upper balcony and feeling that something belonged there, although I had not yet decided what.

I colored the edges, replaced the cat with a dog, replaced the lamppost with a plastic one, and changed several plants to ones I liked better. Otherwise, I did not make many changes.

There were a few odd details. Liquor in the ice bucket did not seem right, so I replaced it with wine. Bottled beer would also have made sense. Liquor bottles beside wine goblets also looked wrong, so I put wine on that table. Highball or shot glasses would have worked better and would have prevented the empty spaces left on the hanging rack.

I let those details go because I was building a model that celebrates my heritage and one of the greatest places I have traveled. It also celebrates my favorite beer and my preferred hard liquor.

This is a large model, but it earned a place of honor on my shelf. Some more books had to go.
