This is my sweet spot: lights, motion, color, and sound. My granddaughter asked for something just for her, and this was my response. I thought she would be pleased, although at six I wondered whether she would recognize it as a television.

Challenge 3/5

This three-star kit has 174 pieces and presented no serious difficulties. It is listed as a three-hour build and took only a little longer than that. I colored some edges, although most did not need it. The model still included several interesting features to figure out.

Enjoyment 5/5

This was simply a sweet kit. It had the gears I love and even a drive belt. Was this really a ROKR model undercover, masquerading as Rolife? I tend to think of the Music Dreamer line as ROKR. The mechanical complexity was there, but it came together so well that it remained a joy to build.

Engineering and Fit 5/5

Exceptional. Nothing got in my way during the build. I was especially pleased with the design, tightness, and stability of the gearbox. It also had some of the best lighting mounts I have seen in a kit.

Materials and Finish 5/5

The wood, MDF, and plastic were high quality throughout. The plastic pieces and the small leather detail on the front were used very effectively. Very little edge coloring was needed because of the viewing angles and strategic placement.

I am obsessive about visible blank edges, and this was a relaxing build because I spent very little time digging through my marker box for matching colors. I especially liked the plastic clouds. As a devoted edge colorer, I vote that all white pieces with visible edges should be plastic in every model. If you know, you know.

Instructions 4/5

I missed having a parts list before each step and may have become too dependent on that feature. I had to go back several times for pieces I overlooked.

It also gives me anxiety when there is no electronics test at the very beginning. I have had electronics problems in only two of 89 builds, and both were easily fixed, but I still worry about getting everything closed up before discovering a problem that requires disassembly.

Display Appeal 5/5

I am a sucker for old-school televisions and also love the Music Dreamer series. With its smart use of plastic and leather, this is a very well-executed nostalgic version of the television from my childhood.

Value 5/5

At under $50, this is an excellent kit for the money. You receive quality materials and a substantial display piece. Compared with what a similarly priced LEGO set provides, I think this comes out well ahead.

Overall Rating 5/5

This is a great kit that my granddaughter should enjoy and be able to keep on her nightstand for a long time.
