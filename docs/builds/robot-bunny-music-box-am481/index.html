I went old school again. I love this series, and this was my third model after the Robot and Robot Dog. It was time to add the bunny to the neighborhood. I also had the space vehicle waiting in my queue and continued searching for the elusive submarine.

I filmed the entire build process for the first, and perhaps only, time. I prefer to immerse myself in a build, and recording everything interrupted the mood. At some point I planned to clean up the video and post it to my YouTube channel.

Challenge 3/5

The box says four stars, but I would call it a three. I had very little difficulty. With only 116 pieces, it was a quick build even with painting.

Enjoyment 5/5

The problems amused me rather than irritated me. I know what I am getting with these older kits and simply go with it. The model required plenty of glue, and I used my mallet once, but that was expected.

Engineering and Fit 3/5

Loose tabs, tight tabs, this kit could not decide what it wanted to be. It did not matter. I own glue, sandpaper, and a mallet. In the end, everything fit.

There is not much rocket science in this series, just a wind-up music box and some spinning gears.

Materials and Finish 5/5

The plywood panels were in surprisingly good condition for an older kit. I had no breaks or splintering. I assume this one was stored properly rather than in the desert garage where some of my other eBay discoveries appear to have lived.

Instructions 3/5

Oh dear. These were not instructions for an inexperienced builder. I often complain about the lack of a parts list before each step, but in this case the parts were not even identified on the boards. There was only a diagram on the second page.

Fortunately, there were not many parts, so I got through it. The diagrams themselves were adequate, but I would not recommend this as anyone's first ROKR kit.

Display Appeal 5/5

It joined my steampunk shelf. I added details from my collection of gears and was very happy with the result.

Value 6/5

My rating system, my rules. I can give it six out of five. I spent an entire $12 on eBay and felt like I stole it.

Overall Rating 5/5

I do not know what a kit from this series would have to do to earn anything less than a five from me. It was a great Sunday-afternoon build, and I am happy to own it.
