Enjoyment 5/5

This was a dream to build. I was in the zone almost the entire time.

Engineering and Fit 5/5

Everything fit and worked like a charm. I found the wiring especially well designed.

Materials and Finish 5/5

The gold-leaf stickers made the finished model look amazing. All the parts were good quality, nothing felt especially fragile, and nothing broke.

Instructions 4/5

I made a few notes. There were places where I added wax even though the instructions did not call for it. If two pieces of moving wood touch, I wax them.

There is also a round piece that locks all the spires into place at the top. I was struggling to keep them aligned, assumed there had to be a solution, and paged ahead until I found it. Installing it early had no negative effect, and after that the side panels went in like a dream.

The wires to the candles created upward pressure on their bases and caused them to pop up, so I glued the bases into place at the end.

Display Appeal 5/5

It is gorgeous. I cannot say it more simply than that.

Value 5/5

This is a model that can come out every Christmas and become part of a family tradition.

Overall Rating 5/5

My German heritage, five generations removed, may make me predisposed to appreciate a replica of a traditional German Christmas decoration. My wife had also seen versions that use real candles to create the rising air that turns the fan and tree, and my mother remembered them from her childhood.

I enjoyed every minute of building this one.
