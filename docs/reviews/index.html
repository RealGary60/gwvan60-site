How I rate kits

I score each kit out of 5 in eight categories. I came up with the system while building a KPOP Demon Hunters 4D kit. Now and then a kit earns a 6. My rating system, my rules.

  1. Challenge
  2. Enjoyment
  3. Engineering and Fit
  4. Materials and Finish
  5. Instructions
  6. Display Appeal
  7. Value
  8. Overall Rating
