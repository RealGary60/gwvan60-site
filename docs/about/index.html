About Gary

I'm Gary VanOpdorp, GWVan60 online. I've built more than 70 wooden and mechanical kits, and over the years the hobby has grown from assembly into sanding, edge coloring, painting, rigging, custom signs, electrical add-ons and repairs.

I film my builds at the bench and write detailed reviews so other builders know what they're getting into. The 29 builds on this site are the ones I've published so far.

In September 2026 I was one of the speakers at Rolife's 9th anniversary celebration at American Dream Mall in New Jersey. Watch my talk.

Away from the bench, I work as a fractional and full-time consultant in data and business analytics.
